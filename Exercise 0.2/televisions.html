Television Energy Consumption

Televisions are one of the most common household appliances in Australia, with an average of 2.3 TVs per household. Understanding their energy consumption can help consumers make informed purchasing decisions.

Typical Energy Consumption by TV Type

  • LED TVs: 30-100 watts (most energy-efficient)
  • LCD TVs: 50-150 watts
  • Plasma TVs: 100-300 watts (least energy-efficient)
  • OLED TVs: 50-150 watts

Tips to Reduce TV Energy Consumption

  • Adjust brightness and contrast settings to optimal levels
  • Enable energy-saving or eco modes
  • Turn off the TV when not in use (avoid standby mode)
  • Consider the TV size - larger screens consume more energy
  • Look for TVs with higher energy star ratings
